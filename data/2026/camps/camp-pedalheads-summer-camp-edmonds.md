## Notes

Pedalheads is a multi-sport camp program offering bike riding, swimming, trail riding, and soccer camps for kids ages 2-8. Their signature learn-to-ride bike camp helps most kids ride independently without training wheels by the end. Programs focus on building confidence and skills through energetic storytelling, interactive games, and personalized instruction. They operate at 250+ locations across Canada and the US with professionally trained instructors. The camp emphasizes safety with background-checked staff, proper equipment checks, and comprehensive safety protocols.

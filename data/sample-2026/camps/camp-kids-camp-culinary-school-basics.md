## Notes

A five-day summer cooking camp where kids ages 8-12 master different culinary techniques each day, culminating in preparing a complete meal with main, side dish and dessert. Students learn proper knife skills, classic sauce preparation, various cooking techniques, and pastry-making science and artistry. The program is hands-on and taught by seasoned culinary instructors. Kids will be ready to cook dinner for the whole family after completing the camp. Available at multiple PCC locations with morning and afternoon time slots.

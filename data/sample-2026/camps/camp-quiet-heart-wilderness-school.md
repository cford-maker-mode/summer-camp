## Notes

Wilderness education program operating since 1997 that focuses on building lifelong connections with nature. Offers diverse camps including forest exploration, primitive skills, stealth and awareness training, nature science, and role-playing adventures. Programs emphasize nature awareness, wilderness skills like fire building and carving, animal tracking, and personal connection through 'Sit Spot' practice. Features small instructor-to-student ratios and operates across multiple park locations in Edmonds, WA area including Yost Park, Pine Ridge Park, and Southwest County Park.

# Camp Catalog Onboarding Copy

Welcome to your summer camp catalog — the place where all the options come together, and you get to make sense of them (without losing yours).

## Camp Catalog Overview (for top of page)
Find summer camps recommended by the community, and add new options by sharing the specific camp session page you’re interested in. Add camps to your Plan to visualize your summer at a glance.
---

## How to Get Started
- Browse the catalog to see camps in your area, with session dates, costs, and key details summarized for easy comparison.
- Use the filters to narrow down by age, grade, location, or special features. The more specific you get, the more relevant your results.
- Click any camp card for a quick summary, or tap "See More" to dig into the full details — registration dates, session schedules, and all the fine print.

## Best Practices for Adding Camps
- When adding a camp, start with the most specific URL you can find — ideally the page for the exact camp location and session dates you want. The more detail on the page, the richer the data we can pull in for you.
- If you only have the general business site, that's fine — but you may need to fill in some blanks yourself. (We know, not every camp makes it easy.)
- Double-check extracted details, especially dates and costs. If something looks off, you can edit it right in the entry — no need to hunt down a support email.

## Getting the Most Value
- Use the catalog to build your summer plan: add camps to your shortlist, compare session dates, and see which options fit your family's schedule.
- Save personal notes or preferences on any camp — your thoughts, reminders, or that one detail you don't want to forget. These are just for you, layered on top of the public info.
- The catalog is designed for clarity: relevant summaries up front, deeper details just a click away. We want you to feel like an insider, not a detective.

## A Little Perspective
Summer camp planning is a thing — and yes, it can get complicated. But with the right info, a little organization, and a catalog that actually works for you, it's a lot more manageable. We're here to help you find the right camps, build a plan, and keep your sanity in the process.
